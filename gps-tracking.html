Service Overview

Know Where Your Fleet Is At All Times

Our GPS Tracking solution gives you complete, real-time visibility over every vehicle in your fleet. Whether you manage a single car, a fleet of boda bodas, or heavy commercial trucks — our system puts you in full control from any device, anywhere in Uganda.

We install compact, tamper-resistant GPS devices on your vehicles and connect them to a live web dashboard and mobile app. You'll see speed, location, routes, stops, and receive instant alerts — all in real time.

Our system is trusted by logistics companies, schools, taxi operators, courier services, and private businesses across Kampala and beyond.

Key Features

Live Real-Time Tracking

Watch your vehicles move on a live map. Updated every few seconds with pinpoint accuracy across Uganda.

Web & Mobile Dashboard

Access your tracking dashboard from any browser or smartphone. Monitor your fleet from anywhere, 24/7.

Instant Alerts

Get SMS or app notifications for overspeeding, unauthorized movement, geo-fence breaches, and ignition events.

Trip History & Reports

Review full movement history, trip summaries, distance covered, and idle time for any vehicle and date range.

Geo-Fence Zones

Define virtual boundaries for your vehicles. Receive instant alerts when a vehicle enters or exits a defined zone.

Theft Protection

Tamper-resistant device installation with hidden wiring. Remote engine immobilization option available on request.

The Process

How It Works

1

Consultation & Assessment

We start with a free consultation to understand your fleet size, vehicle types, and tracking needs. We then recommend the right GPS hardware and plan for your situation.

2

Device Installation

Our certified technicians install the GPS devices on your vehicles — discreetly and securely. Installation is fast, typically 30–60 minutes per vehicle, with no damage to your car's wiring.

3

Dashboard Setup & Training

We set up your online dashboard, create your account, and train you or your team on how to use all the features — live tracking, reports, alerts, and more.

4

Go Live & Ongoing Support

Your fleet is now live and visible. We provide ongoing technical support, device maintenance, and system updates to ensure your tracking never goes offline.
