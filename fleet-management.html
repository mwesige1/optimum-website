Service Overview

Your Fleet, Fully Under Control

Managing a fleet of vehicles without the right tools is expensive, stressful, and risky. Fuel theft, unauthorized trips, poor driving habits, and missed maintenance schedules cost Ugandan businesses millions every year. Our Fleet Management System solves all of these problems in one integrated platform.

We combine GPS tracking hardware with intelligent fleet management software to give you a complete, real-time view of your entire vehicle fleet — from a single dashboard accessible on your phone or computer.

Whether you run a taxi company, a delivery business, a school bus service, or a corporate transport fleet, our system scales to fit your operation and delivers measurable cost savings from day one.

What's Included

System Features

Real-Time Vehicle Tracking

Live map view of all your vehicles. See exact location, speed, direction, and status of every vehicle in your fleet at any moment.

Driver Behavior Monitoring

Track speeding, harsh braking, rapid acceleration, and idle time. Generate driver scorecards to identify and correct risky behaviors before accidents happen.

Fuel Consumption Reports

Monitor fuel usage per vehicle, per trip, and over time. Identify excessive consumption, fuel theft, and opportunities to reduce your fuel bill significantly.

Maintenance Scheduling

Set automated maintenance reminders based on mileage or time intervals. Never miss an oil change, service, or inspection again — keeping your fleet in top condition.

Route Optimization

Plan and assign efficient routes for your drivers. Reduce travel time, fuel costs, and vehicle wear by ensuring every trip takes the most optimal path.

Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive fleet performance reports — distance covered, trips completed, costs incurred, and driver rankings — all in one clear, exportable dashboard.
