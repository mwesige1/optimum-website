Service Overview

Connected Spaces, Happy Customers

Our Oil Hotspot Services provide end-to-end WiFi hotspot solutions for businesses that want to offer internet access to their customers and staff. We handle everything — from hardware installation to branded captive portal design and ongoing management.

Whether you run a petrol station, a restaurant, a hotel, a waiting lounge, or a shared office space, we'll deploy a reliable, scalable hotspot system that enhances your customer experience.

Our systems include user authentication, bandwidth management, and remote monitoring — giving you full control over who connects and how much they use.

What's Included

Hotspot Features

Full Coverage Installation

We install access points strategically to ensure strong, consistent WiFi signal across your entire premises.

Branded Captive Portal

A custom login page with your business logo and colors that users see before connecting — great for branding and marketing.

User Access Control

Manage who connects and for how long. Set voucher-based access, time limits, or open access depending on your preference.

Bandwidth Management

Set speed limits per user to ensure fair usage and prevent any one user from consuming all available bandwidth.

Usage Monitoring

Track connected users, bandwidth consumption, and peak usage times through a central management dashboard.

Remote Management & Support

We monitor your hotspot system remotely and resolve any issues quickly — often before you even notice a problem.
