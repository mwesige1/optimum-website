Service Overview

Brand Your Team, Impress Everyone

A well-branded team is a professional team. Whether you're equipping your staff with branded uniforms or producing promotional merchandise for an event or campaign, our embroidery service delivers quality you can see — and feel — in every stitch.

We use modern computerized embroidery machines that produce sharp, accurate, and durable designs. Your company logo or artwork is digitized to perfection and stitched onto garments with precise thread color matching and consistent quality across every piece in your order.

We handle orders of all sizes — from a single piece to bulk orders of hundreds of units — all delivered on time and to the highest standard. We supply a range of blank garments or work with items you provide.

The Process

How It Works

1

Design Submission

Share your logo or artwork with us in any format — JPG, PNG, PDF, or AI. If you don't have a logo yet, our design team can create one for you before we proceed to embroidery.

2

Digitization & Quoting

We digitize your artwork into an embroidery file, select the correct thread colors, and provide you with a detailed quote covering garment supply, digitization, and stitching costs.

3

Sample Approval

Before producing the full order, we stitch a sample piece for your review and approval. We make any adjustments to sizing, placement, or thread colors based on your feedback.

4

Production & Delivery

Once approved, we produce your full order efficiently and to the highest standard. We package everything neatly and deliver to your location or arrange collection at our office.
